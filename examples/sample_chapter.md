# Chapter 1. The Painter at the Gate

The dungeon gate was older than the city. Mira Holt set her easel in front of it anyway, because the light was good and nobody else wanted the spot.

"You're not supposed to be here," said the guard. He was young, and his spear looked heavier than he did.

"I'm not going in," Mira said. "I'm only painting the door."

"People who paint the door usually end up going in."

She dipped her brush in ash-grey and drew the first line. The stone under her brush *moved*.

The guard dropped his spear. Mira did not stop painting.

"Well," she said quietly, "that's new."
